Who gets the ride?

The same riders and drivers run through two engines. Greedy gives each rider the nearest driver in arrival order. Batched collects requests for 2 seconds, then solves one optimal assignment (Hungarian algorithm), weighing wait time, surge priority and each driver's chance of accepting.
